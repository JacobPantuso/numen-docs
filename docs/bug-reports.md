# Bug Reports

<p align="center">
  <img src="../images/bug-reports.png" width="240" alt="Bug Reports in Settings">
</p>

**Settings › Bug Reports** lists issues reported from the app, both open and recently resolved. If an issue is not already listed, **Report a Bug** submits a new one, which is filed as a GitHub issue.

## Diagnostics

Each report includes the app and iOS versions, the iPhone and vehicle models, the state of the vehicle and adapters at the time of the report, and the app's recent activity log. Diagnostics are always attached, as they are required to investigate most issues.

## Redaction

Before a report leaves the device, information that could identify the user or vehicle is removed, including the VIN, account details, email address, PIN, place names, adapter names and locations.
